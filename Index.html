Request account
and data deletion.

This page is for AfriGo users who want to request deletion of their AfriGo account and the personal data associated with it. You can make a request here without reinstalling or opening the AfriGo app.

Start a deletion request

Complete the details below to prepare an email to the AfriGo team. Your email application will open with the request ready for you to review and send.

We will use this address to respond to your request.
AfriGo guest accounts may not have an email address attached.
Never send your password, one-time verification code, full payment-card number, or other login secrets. AfriGo may contact you to verify that you control the account before processing a request.

What happens next

  • Send your request to the AfriGo team using the email draft.
  • We may ask for reasonable information to verify the account and protect it from unauthorised deletion.
  • Once verified, AfriGo will process deletion of the account and associated data held in AfriGo systems.

Important about bookings

Deleting an AfriGo account does not automatically cancel a reservation with an airline, hotel, activity provider or other travel supplier, and does not itself issue a refund. Contact the relevant provider for changes, cancellations or refunds. Certain records may be retained where required for legitimate legal, security or fraud-prevention reasons; any applicable retention will be explained to you.
