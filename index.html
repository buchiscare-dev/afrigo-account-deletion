Verification, Processing & Retention

  • Ownership Verification: To protect your privacy and prevent unauthorized removal, AfriGo verifies account ownership via email or matching account identifiers before deleting records.
  • What Is Deleted: Once verified, we permanently delete or anonymize your AfriGo account profile, travel preferences, saved destinations, Astra conversation links, and device installation records associated with your account.
  • Legally Required Retention: Limited records may be retained strictly as required or permitted by applicable law—for example, tax, accounting, payment dispute, security, or fraud-prevention obligations.

Bookings, Refunds & Subscriptions

Deleting an AfriGo account does not automatically cancel third-party bookings or generate refunds. Any hotels, flights, experiences, or transport reserved through external travel providers remain subject to those providers' own cancellation and refund policies.

Deleting your AfriGo account also does not automatically cancel an active Google Play subscription. Manage or cancel subscriptions directly at play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions.
